Working the numbers…

What a repeat agent is worth

One campaign at $49 has high margin and still cannot pay for a customer, because acquisition has to come out of that single sale. An agent who lists several homes a year and runs several campaigns on each is worth several campaigns — which is the only arithmetic that makes paid acquisition work.
