Data & privacy

Delete your data.

Last updated November 14, 2026

Your data is yours. You can delete your Resmara account and everything in it at any time. We finish deleting your data within 30 days, and nothing comes back after that.

Option 1: Close your account in the app

  1. Sign in at resmara.com.
  2. Open Billing in the menu.
  3. Click Close my account and confirm.

This stops any scheduled posts, disconnects Meta and starts deleting your data straight away.

Option 2: Email us

Send a request from the email address on your account. We will confirm by email when your data has been deleted.

Email a deletion request

Option 3: Remove Resmara from Facebook

  1. In Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations.
  2. Find Resmara and click Remove.
  3. To also delete what Resmara holds, use Option 1 or 2 above.

Removing Resmara in Facebook stops our access to your Pages, Instagram and ad accounts immediately.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and login
  • Your brand kit: logo, headshot, colors, fonts and brand notes
  • Listing details, uploaded photos and videos, and every caption, image and ad we created
  • Your Meta access token and the list of Pages, Instagram accounts and ad accounts you connected
  • Campaign reports and the performance numbers we collected from Meta
  • Support conversations

What we keep, and why

  • Purchase records (date, amount, last four card digits) for as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Posts and ads already published stay on your Facebook Page, Instagram account and ad account, because they belong to you. You can delete them there at any time.
  • Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis and are fully cleared within 30 days.
