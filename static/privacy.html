Data & privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated November 14, 2026

The short version. Resmara turns your listing into a 14-day marketing campaign. We keep the account and property details you give us, we use the Facebook, Instagram and Meta ad accounts you connect only to publish, run and report on campaigns you approve, and we never sell your data.

Who this covers

This policy explains how Resmara (“Resmara”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and deletes information when you visit resmara.com or use the Resmara app. Resmara is a business tool for real estate professionals.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a password hash. We never store the password itself.
  • Brand details: your logo, headshot, brokerage, colors, fonts and the public brand information we gather from your website and social profiles to build your brand kit.
  • Listing content: property addresses, facts, descriptions, photos and videos you upload or link to, and the captions, images and ads we create from them.
  • Meta data: when you connect Meta, see the next section.
  • Payments: Stripe processes card payments. We receive a payment confirmation and the last four digits of your card. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Service records: campaign status, support requests and technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and error details) used to keep the service working and secure.

Information we receive from Meta

When you click Connect Meta you sign in with Facebook and choose which Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and ad accounts Resmara may use. Meta then gives us an access token. Here is each permission we ask for and why:

PermissionWhat Resmara uses it for
pages_show_listShows the Facebook Pages you manage so you can pick the one for your campaign.
pages_read_engagementReads your Page name, picture and posts so your campaign matches your Page and we can confirm posts went out.
pages_manage_postsPublishes the listing posts you approved to your Page on the scheduled days.
pages_manage_adsRuns your listing ad as your Page.
instagram_basicFinds the Instagram professional account linked to your Page and shows its name and picture.
instagram_content_publishPublishes the listing posts you approved to your Instagram account.
ads_managementCreates and manages the listing ad campaign, using the budget and dates you confirmed, in your own ad account.
ads_readReads your ad's results (reach, clicks, spend) for your day 7 and day 14 reports.
read_insightsReads how your Page posts performed for your reports.
instagram_manage_insightsReads how your Instagram posts performed for your reports.
business_managementLets you choose a Page or ad account that belongs to your Meta Business portfolio.

We only use Meta data to provide the Resmara features you asked for. We do not sell it, use it for anyone else’s advertising, or use it to build profiles of the people who see or engage with your posts and ads. Your access token is stored encrypted on our servers, never in your browser, and is deleted when you disconnect or close your account.

How we use your information

  • To build your brand kit and the campaign drafts you review.
  • To publish the posts and run the ad you approved, on the dates and budget you set.
  • To prepare your day 7 and day 14 seller reports.
  • To take payment, answer support requests, prevent fraud and keep the service running.
  • To send you emails about your account and campaigns. We do not send marketing emails without your consent.

Who we share it with

  • Meta Platforms receives the posts, ad creative, budget and targeting you approved so they can be published under your accounts.
  • Stripe processes payments.
  • AI model providers (such as OpenAI) receive the property facts, photos and brand details needed to write copy and create images. Under their business terms they do not use this data to train their models.
  • Hosting and email providers that run our servers and send account emails, under contracts that limit them to providing that service.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or Resmara.

We never sell or rent your personal information.

Cookies and browser storage

Resmara uses a sign-in cookie and your browser’s local storage to keep you signed in and remember your settings. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking on resmara.com.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, brand and campaign records while your account is open. When you close your account, or ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days, as described on our data deletion page. We keep purchase records for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect Meta at any time from Accounts in the app, or remove Resmara in your Facebook settings under Business integrations.
  • Access, correct or download your information by emailing us.
  • Delete your account and data. See Data Deletion.
  • California residents can ask what personal information we hold about them and ask us to delete or correct it. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access tokens and passwords are stored on our servers, never in your browser, and access to production data is limited to the people who run the service. No system is perfectly secure, but we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your data.

Children

Resmara is for real estate professionals and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
