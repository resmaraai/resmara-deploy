Data & privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version. Resmara turns a listing into a marketing campaign for real estate professionals. We keep the account and property details you give us, use the Facebook, Instagram and Meta ad accounts you connect only to run and report on your campaigns, collect leads from your listing websites and posts on your behalf, and never sell your data.

Who this covers

This policy explains how Resmara (“Resmara”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and deletes personal information. It covers three groups of people:

  • Agents: real estate professionals who use the Resmara app.
  • Visitors to resmara.com, our own website.
  • Visitors to listing websites we host for agents (such as homes.resmara.com), and people who comment on an agent's posts or ads.

For listing websites and leads, we collect information on behalf of the agent whose listing it is. That agent decides how to follow up with you, and their own privacy practices apply to what they do with your information once they have it.

Information from agents

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number, real estate license number, brokerage, and the details of your Facebook sign-in.
  • Brand details: your logo, headshot, colors, fonts, voice and the public information we gather about your business from your website, your social profiles and public listing sites to build your brand kit.
  • Listing details: addresses, property facts, descriptions, prices, photos, videos and listing links you give us, the public information we gather about the property, and the posts, images, websites and ads we create from them.
  • Your feedback and edits: thumbs up or down, edits and approvals, which teach Resmara your style.
  • Payments: Stripe processes card payments. We receive confirmation of each payment and the card's brand, expiry and last four digits. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Support: messages to us, the Ask Resmara help chat and setup calls.
  • Usage and device data: pages and screens you use, clicks, IP address, browser and device type, and error logs (see “Cookies and tracking”).

Information we receive from Meta

When you connect Meta you sign in with Facebook and choose which Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and ad accounts Resmara may use. Meta then gives us an access token. Here is each permission we ask for and why:

PermissionWhat Resmara uses it for
pages_show_listShows the Facebook Pages you manage so you can pick the one for your campaign.
pages_read_engagementReads your Page's name, picture and posts, and the comments on the posts and ad Resmara published for you: to match your campaign to your Page, confirm posts went out, spot comments from people interested in the home, and notice when one of your own posts says a listing went pending, sold or changed price.
pages_manage_postsPublishes the listing posts you approved to your Page on the scheduled days.
pages_manage_adsRuns your listing ad as your Page.

read_insightsReads how your Page posts performed.
instagram_manage_insightsReads how your Instagram posts performed.

We only use Meta data to provide the Resmara features you use. We don't sell it, use it for anyone else's advertising, or use it to build profiles of the people who see or engage with your posts and ads. Your access token is stored encrypted on our servers, never in your browser, and is deleted when you disconnect or close your account. Only your business Page and Instagram professional account are read, never a personal profile.

Information about buyers, renters and other leads

If you visit a listing website we host, or comment on an agent's listing post or ad, we may collect:

  • What you send: your name, email address, phone number and message when you use a contact form.
  • Taps to call or text: that you tapped to call or text the agent, and when. We don't record the call or read the text.
  • Comments: your public display name and the text of comments on the posts and ad Resmara published for the agent, so we can flag people who seem interested in the home.
  • Visits: pages viewed, how you arrived (for example from the agent's ad or a shared link), approximate location from your IP address, and your browser and device type.

We use this only to pass your interest on to the agent, show the agent how their listing is doing, and keep the website working and secure. We don't message you ourselves, sell your information, or use it to advertise other homes or services to you. Seller reports show only counts, never names. Lead records are kept as long as the agent's campaign and account exist (removed leads are kept marked “removed” so they aren't flagged again), and are deleted with the campaign or the account. To ask about your information, contact the agent or email us.

How we use information

  • To provide the Service: research listings, build brand kits, campaigns and listing websites, publish what agents approve, run ads, collect leads, check listing status and price, and prepare reports.
  • To take payments, including automatic renewals that an agent has turned on.
  • To send service emails about accounts, campaigns, renewals, leads, price changes and reports.
  • To send agents tips and updates, if they agreed at sign-up (see “Emails”).
  • To measure and improve Resmara, including our own ads and website, and to improve how Resmara writes and designs. Where we use campaign results and feedback this way, we use them in aggregated or de-identified form wherever we can.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and abuse, keep the Service secure, enforce our Terms and meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only as described here:

  • The agent, for leads and listing website activity on their listings.
  • Meta Platforms, which receives the posts, ad creative, budget and targeting the agent approved so they can be published under the agent's accounts.
  • Service providers that work for us under contracts limiting what they can do with the data:
    • Stripe (payments)
    • Render (hosting and database)
    • Cloudflare (photo storage)
    • Resend and Google Workspace (service emails)
    • Mailchimp (marketing emails to agents)
    • AI model providers such as OpenAI, which receive property facts, photos and brand details needed to research, write and design. Under their business terms they don't use this data to train their models.
    • Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (website and app analytics)
    • Calendly (setup calls)
  • Advertising measurement: resmara.com uses the Meta Pixel to measure and improve Resmara's own ads (see below).
  • Business changes: if Resmara is sold, merged or reorganized, information may transfer to the new owner under this policy.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our users, the public or Resmara.

We never sell personal information for money.

Cookies and tracking

  • Essential: a sign-in cookie and your browser's local storage keep you signed in and remember your settings.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (with IP anonymization) and Microsoft Clarity measure how resmara.com, the app and listing websites are used. Clarity records clicks, scrolls and page movements so we can see where people get stuck; inside the app it's linked to the signed-in agent's email so we can help with support. Typed passwords and payment details aren't recorded.
  • Advertising: resmara.com (our own site, not listing websites) uses the Meta Pixel to learn which of our ads bring sign-ups and to show our ads to people who visited. Under California law this may count as “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Your choice. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as a request to opt out, and the Meta Pixel won't load. You can also opt out on this browser here:

Done. The Meta Pixel won't load on this browser.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

Emails

Service emails (account, payments, renewals, campaigns, leads, price changes, reports) are part of the Service and go to every agent while their account is open. Tips and updates are sent through Mailchimp only to agents who agreed at sign-up, and every one has an unsubscribe link. You can also turn them off in your account settings. Mailchimp tells us whether those emails are opened and clicked, which helps us see what's useful.

How long we keep it

We keep agent accounts, brand kits, campaigns, listing websites and leads while the account is open. When an account is closed, or when you ask us to delete your data, we delete it within 30 days, as described on our data deletion page. We keep payment records as long as tax and accounting laws require, and may keep information longer where the law requires it or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Aggregated or de-identified data may be kept.

Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect Meta at any time from Accounts in the app, or remove Resmara in your Facebook settings under Business integrations.
  • Access, correct, download or delete your information by emailing us, or delete your account in the app (see Data Deletion).
  • Unsubscribe from tips and updates with the link in any of those emails.
  • Opt out of ad sharing with the button above or a Global Privacy Control signal.

California residents. Under the California Consumer Privacy Act you can ask what personal information we've collected about you (the categories described above: identifiers, commercial information, internet activity, approximate location, and professional information) and how we use and share it; ask us to delete or correct it; and opt out of “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising. We don't sell personal information, and we don't use or disclose sensitive personal information beyond what's needed to provide the Service. We won't treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, email us; we'll verify it by confirming you control the email address on file, and you may use an authorized agent. If you're a buyer or renter who contacted an agent, we may also pass your request to that agent.

People in other states may have similar rights, and we'll honor them as the law requires.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Access tokens are encrypted, passwords are never stored in plain text, payment cards are handled by Stripe, and access to production data is limited to the people who run the Service. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we'll notify you as the law requires.

Children

Resmara is for real estate professionals and isn't intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell agents in the app or by email before they take effect.
